# 📊 Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales and Profit Analysis Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, profitability, product categories, customer segments, and regional sales distribution.

It helps users understand business performance through interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs), supporting data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Track total orders, quantity sold, and customer count.
- Compare sales performance across regions and product categories.
- Identify profitable and underperforming product sub-categories.
- Analyze sales distribution across customer segments.
- Explore sales trends over time using interactive charts and filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and cleaning, where applicable
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs, where applicable
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, graphs, and KPI cards

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales and Profit Trend
Visualizes sales and profit trends over time to help identify fluctuations and patterns.

### 2. Sales by Category
Compares sales performance across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.

### 3. Regional Sales Analysis
Displays the distribution of sales across the Central, East, South, and West regions.

### 4. Profit by Sub-Category
Compares profit performance across product sub-categories to identify stronger and weaker contributors.

### 5. Sales by Customer Segment
Analyzes sales distribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### 6. Interactive Filters
Allows users to explore the dashboard using filters for:
- Region
- Category
- Segment
- Order Date
